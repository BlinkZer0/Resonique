# Resonique: privacy policy

*Effective 21 November 2026. Applies to the Resonique Android app (com.blinkzer0.resonique), the Resonique VST3
plugin and Standalone app for Windows, and the Resonique web build. The hosted copy is docs/privacy.html.*

Resonique does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** The app's interface and sound engine are part of the app and run entirely on your device.
  It doesn't contact any server, and the Android app requests no internet permission.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** No advertising ID is used.
- **MIDI.** The app reads MIDI messages from MIDI devices you connect (USB or Bluetooth), only to play sound. They
  are not recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Your presets and settings** are saved inside the app on your device. If you use Android's backup, they're
  included in your own Google backup like other app data; the developer has no access to it. On Windows, presets
  are files in your Documents folder.
- **Files you open** (preset, Scala tuning or impulse-response files) are read once to load them and are not kept or
  sent anywhere. Files you export are written where you choose.
- **Children.** The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy will be published at the same address with a new effective date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/BlinkZer0/Resonique/issues.
